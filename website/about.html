About Stay Cayman

Your gateway to the finest boutique stays on Grand Cayman

Our Story

Stay Cayman was born from a simple belief: that every traveler deserves more than a room with a view. Founded by a team of island locals and hospitality veterans, we set out to curate Grand Cayman's most exceptional boutique hotel offerings — the kinds of places that turn a holiday into a lifelong memory. From intimate beachfront retreats to design-forward hideaways tucked along the coastline, every property in our collection has been personally vetted and chosen for its character, quality, and sense of place.

But we don't stop at accommodations. Grand Cayman is an island rich with hidden coves, world-class dive sites, and a culinary scene that rivals any Caribbean destination. We work hand-in-hand with local guides, chefs, and artisans to offer experiences you simply won't find anywhere else — private sunset sails, farm-to-table dinners on the sand, and snorkeling excursions to untouched reefs. Our mission is to connect visitors with the authentic heart of island life, one unforgettable stay at a time.
