Grand Cayman is a year-round destination with average temperatures between 77°F and 88°F. The peak season runs from December through April, offering the driest weather and coolest breezes — ideal for beach days and diving. The shoulder months of May, June, and November offer excellent value with fewer crowds. Even during the summer rainy season, showers are typically brief and followed by sunshine.
Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM) in George Town serves direct flights from major U.S. cities including Miami, New York (JFK), Charlotte, Dallas, and Atlanta, as well as Toronto and London. Flight times from the U.S. East Coast are typically 3–4 hours. Several major airlines operate routes including Cayman Airways, American Airlines, JetBlue, United, and British Airways. We can also arrange private airport transfers to your hotel.
Yes, a valid passport is required for all visitors entering the Cayman Islands. U.S., Canadian, and U.K. citizens do not need a visa for stays up to 30 days. Make sure your passport is valid for at least six months beyond your planned departure date. We recommend checking the latest entry requirements with the Cayman Islands government website before your trip, as policies can change.
